What it covers

On Oct 25, 2026, Thorsten Ball (who works on the Amp coding agent) published the X article "How I work with Agents… as of October 2026," a list of 15 rules he'd been sending friends in DMs. It got more than 11,000 bookmarks. This episode goes through every rule, the replies where he explained them, and the pushback.

  • Use the best model, prompt the "what" and not the "how," and make the whole software lifecycle visible to the agent.
  • Ask agents for proof, write down what doesn't count as evidence, test your AGENTS.md with control vs. treatment, and let agents build their own tools for iterating.
  • The debate on X: cost and compaction (@codydotjs, @Drk8_), keeping read and write access to production separate (@subnetmask), and reviewing only code with a high blast radius (@elissasa, @lilianakastilio).
